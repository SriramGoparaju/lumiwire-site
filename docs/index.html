Connect every wire. Fill every cell.

Lumiwire is a calm circuit puzzle. Drag a wire between matching sockets, never cross another wire, and cover the whole board. Every board has exactly one solution, and you never have to guess.

A long Journey for the evenings, one Daily board shared by everyone, and a one-minute Blitz for the queue at the bank.

See it

Graphite when it's dark, Paper when it's light. The app follows your phone, or you can choose.

Made to be solved, not fought

One answer, always

Every board is proven by a solver to have exactly one solution, with every obstacle in place. Nothing is hidden and nothing is random, so careful thinking always gets you there.

Eight chapters, eight new ideas

Dead chips, cut traces, fuses, relays, soldered traces, bridges, vias and wrap-around edges, each introduced gently on an easy board with a one-line tip.

Stars for style, not speed

Draw every wire in one stroke for three stars. In the Journey the clock never counts.

A Daily that doesn't nag

One board a day, the same for everyone, with a leaderboard if you want it. No streak counter on the home screen, and nothing lost for missing a day.

Blitz when you have a minute

Sixty seconds of tiny boards. A board drawn in one stroke per wire adds two seconds. Daily and weekly leaderboards for the competitive.

Easy on the eyes

Graphite in the dark, Paper in the light. Every pair has a symbol as well as a colour, and the colours are tuned to stay distinct for colour-blind players.

Plays offline

Everything plays without a connection. Your results go up to the leaderboards when you're back online.

No sign-up

No account to make and no email to give. Open it and play.
